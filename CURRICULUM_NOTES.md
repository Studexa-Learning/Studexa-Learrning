# Curriculum mapping notes

The prototype's subject structure was informed by current Pearson materials checked on 29 Sep 2026:

- Pearson Edexcel GCSE Mathematics (1MA1): six content areas — Number; Algebra; Ratio, proportion and rates of change; Geometry and measures; Probability; Statistics.
- Pearson Edexcel GCSE Combined Science (1SC0): biology, chemistry and physics topic structure from the current specification.
- Pearson Maths Progress for KS3: Year 7–9 progression and topic sequencing.

The generated Studexa topic names and homework records are original metadata and are not copied assessment questions.
